01 · Executive summary

A credible digital campus for students and guardians.

VardexLabs proposes to design, develop and deploy a responsive institutional website for U40 Academy Inn. The website will communicate academic programs, residential facilities, student outcomes and admissions information through a clear, modern experience designed for mobile-first visitors.

Primary project objectives

  • Present U40 Academy Inn as a credible residential science institution.
  • Make academic programs, hostel facilities and results easy to understand.
  • Provide one clear path from discovery to an admission inquiry.

Complete website experience, from strategy to launch.

The engagement covers the design and engineering required to deliver a production-ready institutional website—not a generic template or a static brochure page.

01

05

06 · Terms & assumptions

Clear responsibilities for a smooth delivery.

Proposal validity

This commercial proposal remains valid for 15 calendar days from 02 August 2026. Scope and pricing may be reviewed after expiry.

Project commencement

Work begins after written approval, receipt of the 40% advance and availability of the essential client content.

Feedback & approvals

The client will provide consolidated feedback through one nominated representative. Delayed feedback may move delivery dates.

Change requests

Requirements outside the approved scope will be estimated separately and started only after written approval of cost and schedule.

Intellectual property

Upon full payment, the client receives ownership of the custom project source and final design deliverables, excluding third-party tools and licences.

Warranty support

The 30-day warranty covers reproducible defects in delivered scope. New features, new content and third-party changes are excluded.

Hosting & domain

Deployment configuration is included. Domain registration, renewal and any paid Cloudflare or third-party usage charges remain the client’s responsibility.

Content responsibility

The client is responsible for the accuracy, ownership and legal permission of all institutional claims, results, names, photos and policy content.

Not included unless separately agreed

  • Custom admin dashboard or staff login portal
  • Online fee payment gateway or accounting integration
  • Student learning management system, attendance or examination portal
  • Professional photography, video production or extensive copywriting
  • Bulk historical data entry, migration or document digitisation
  • Ongoing SEO campaigns, advertising, social media or maintenance retainers
