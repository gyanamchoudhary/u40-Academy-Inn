# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective students in Classes IX–XII and their parents or guardians, primarily in and around Malda, who are comparing residential science education, coaching, and hostel support. Visitors need to understand the academic model, daily residential care, outcomes, facilities, and admission process with confidence.

## Product Purpose

Present U40 Academy Inn as a disciplined residential science institute where teaching, guided study, accommodation, meals, and mentorship work as one system. The website should help families judge fit, plan a campus visit, and submit an admission inquiry.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, reassuring, and purposeful. The voice is direct and specific, with an academic tone that still feels attentive to students living away from home.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic coaching-centre templates, decorative education clichés, emoji iconography, excessive rounded cards, weak contrast, invisible focus states, instant visual state changes, and layouts that feel playful or luxurious at the expense of academic credibility.

## Design Principles

1. Show the complete student routine, not only classroom claims.
2. Make care tangible through specific facilities, meals, schedules, and support.
3. Give evidence and outcomes visual priority over vague promotional language.
4. Keep admission actions obvious without making the page feel aggressive.
5. Maintain a consistent, structured rhythm that reflects campus discipline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, descriptive image alternatives, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly targets, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow from 375px upward. Interactions must remain usable without hover.
